Our Mission

Impact4Kidz supports education and workforce development by creating technology programs and services that provide high school and college students with practical experience in IT, cybersecurity, software development, and related fields. We also provide fundraising tools and affordable technology services to educational programs, including reduced-cost access for organizations serving low-income and underserved communities.

Learn. Build. Support.

Our work connects practical technology experience with public benefit. Students learn by contributing to real projects, and those projects help support educational programs and communities.

Learn

High school and college interns gain supervised, practical experience in information technology, cybersecurity, software development, cloud services, and related professional skills.

Build

Students contribute to real technology platforms and services designed around real users, real operational needs, and responsible engineering practices.

Support

Our technology helps educational clubs and programs raise funds and access services. Programs serving low-income and underserved communities may receive reduced-cost or subsidized access.

Projects

Impact4Kidz projects are vehicles for education, professional experience, and support for educational communities.

GradVids

Fundraising with a personal touch

GradVids is a DBA of Impact4Kidz and the name of our fundraising platform. It enables supporters to make donations while sending custom, personalized congratulatory video messages to recent graduates. The platform helps educational clubs and programs raise money for their activities.

Parkoach

Real-world technology experience

Parkoach is being developed as a practical technology project that gives students hands-on exposure to modern software development, cloud infrastructure, security, data, and product operations. Its development helps support Impact4Kidz's broader educational and workforce-development mission.

About Impact4Kidz

Impact4Kidz is a nonprofit organization focused on education, workforce development, and access to technology-enabled fundraising and services for educational programs.

Practical experience

We create opportunities for students to work on real systems, solve real problems, and build professional experience before entering the workforce.

Educational support

We provide tools and services that help educational clubs, programs, and planning committees support their own missions and activities.

Accessible by design

Whenever practical, we reduce or subsidize fees for programs serving low-income and underserved communities, with nominal charges used only to help cover infrastructure costs.
